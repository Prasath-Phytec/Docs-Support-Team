BSP Selection and Fundamentals
==============================

This guide explains how to choose the right PHYTEC BSP for your board and requirements, using a compatible release. It introduces the Linux kernel, bootloader (U-Boot or Barebox) and Yocto in simple terms, and shows which of them to change for a given requirement. It also gives a short overview of the NXP, TI and PHYTEC pinmux tools, with NXP and TI examples throughout.
